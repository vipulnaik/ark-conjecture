# Research process

The project started through a chat session between Vipul Naik and Claude Fable 5, where Vipul asked Claude to look at BBKN and come up with a good abstraction, then push the number theory as far as possible. After initially claiming the Chowla-based n^{3/2} as best, Fable eventually came up with a version of the construction we finally used, and brought Hardy-Littlewood/Bateman-Horn into play.

The research was developed, with several documents of working notes, scripts, and output files created and modified in the process.

* Throughout the development process (prior to final writing, which was human) all document edits were done by Claude, based on high-level research direction by Vipul.

* Fable was used for the initial breakthrough and some of the harder lemmas, but Opus was used for most of the routine editing. Back-and-forth switching was done within chat sessions.

* Vipul used several chat sessions in sequence, starting most nee chat sessions with a similar prompt that asked for a thorough review of existing documents.

* Vipul's role was limited too e similar to a mentor/adviser, identifying what lemmas were likely provable through what kind of strategy, identifying high-level inconsistency, identifying categories of errors by eyeballing to find examples and guiding on the direction to take the research next.

* As the rate of mistakes caught in each new session decreased enough that the results felt more robust, Vipul spent more effort mastering the individual details if the construction and proofs.
